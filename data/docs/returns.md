# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused and in their original packaging. Final sale items and gift cards cannot be returned.

## How to start a return
Start a return from the Orders page in your account, or ask support with your order id. We email a prepaid return label for orders in the United States.

## Refund timing
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after we receive the return. Shipping costs are refunded only if the item arrived damaged or was wrong.

## Damaged or wrong items
If an item arrives damaged or is not what you ordered, contact us within 7 days with a photo. We send a replacement or a full refund, including shipping.
