# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days within the United States. Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 PM Eastern ship the same business day.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free on orders over $50. Below $50 it costs $5.99. Express shipping costs $14.99 on any order.

## International shipping
We ship to Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. International delivery takes 7 to 14 business days. Import duties and taxes are paid by the customer on delivery.

## Tracking an order
You get a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships. You can also ask this assistant for the status of an order using its order id, for example ORD-1042.
