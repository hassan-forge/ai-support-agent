# Billing and payments

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and Apple Pay. We do not accept checks or bank transfers for online orders.

## Invoices
Business customers can download a PDF invoice for every order from the Orders page. Invoices show the company name and tax id entered at checkout.

## Sales tax
Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the delivery address. Tax-exempt organizations can send their exemption certificate to support to have it applied to future orders.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, the order is held for 48 hours so you can update your card. After 48 hours the order is cancelled automatically.
